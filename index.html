IPTV buffering on Virgin Media is usually caused by network throttling or device settings. You can often fix it in under five minutes without changing your subscription.

Try the solutions below, from fastest to most effective.

Causes and fixes

Cause: Virgin Media is slowing your IPTV traffic → Fix: Switch to Cloudflare DNS

Virgin Media, like many ISPs, can throttle streaming data. Changing your DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) bypasses this restriction. It’s a safe, free change.

  • Go to your device’s network settings.
  • Change DNS to 1.1.1.1 (and 1.0.0.1 as secondary).
  • Restart your IPTV app.

This fix works on all devices listed in the fact sheet: Fire TV Stick, Android TV Box, Samsung Smart TV, LG webOS, Xiaomi Mi Box S, and Apple TV.

Cause: Your player’s buffer is too low → Fix: Set buffer to 5,000 ms

A low buffer causes frequent pauses. Premium services like SubTV IP recommend a 5,000 ms buffer. This setting is available in most IPTV players.

  • Open TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, or IPTV Smarters Pro.
  • Go to playback or buffer settings.
  • Set buffer size to 5,000 ms (or “Large”).
  • Save and restart the stream.

Cause: Your internet connection is unstable → Fix: Use Ethernet or move closer

WiFi on Virgin Media can drop packets, especially in crowded homes. A wired Ethernet connection gives the most stable speed. If you must use WiFi, move your device closer to the router.

  • Plug your TV box or Fire Stick directly into the router with an Ethernet cable.
  • If using WiFi, turn off Bluetooth on nearby devices.
  • Test with a speed check from Consumer Reports (they recommend at least 25 Mbps for 4K).

Cause: Your IPTV app is outdated → Fix: Update to the latest version

Old apps can’t handle modern HEVC streams or anti-freeze servers. The SubTV IP service uses 4K 60 fps HEVC streams, which need recent player versions.

  • Update Kodi 21, Flex IPTV, or your current app to the latest build.
  • If you use SubTV IP, you can also contact their WhatsApp support for app-specific tips.

Cause: Virgin Media’s router is overloaded → Fix: Restart your router or enable QoS

Router memory fills up after days of heavy use. A simple reboot clears it. For advanced users, enable Quality of Service (QoS) in your Virgin Media router settings to prioritise streaming traffic.

  1. Unplug your Virgin Media router for 30 seconds.
  2. Plug it back in and wait 2 minutes.
  3. Go to router settings (usually 192.168.0.1) and look for QoS.
  4. Set IPTV traffic as high priority.
